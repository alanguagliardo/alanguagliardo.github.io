About us

Since 2021, The Guagliardo Group has helped and empowered clients to overcome their most challenging obstacles and achieve their goals. The group has served in strategic business planning, business development, merger and acquisition integration, app development, EMR ecosystem selection and implementation, MIPS five star planning, and overall operations for healthcare clients.

Founded by Alan Guagliardo, the group taps into his experience and the combined expertise of executives from great companies such as DaVita, Acuity Eye Group, and Pacific Dental Services. Prior to founding the company, Alan served as right hand to the founder and CEO at Acuity and then went on to lead a dental startup to a critical investment round.

He started his career on the clinical innovation team at DaVita, where the small and scrappy Sea Otters turned moonshots into pilots and ultimately into scalable programs. He had the luck to have great mentors and leaders that he followed through the dialysis, hospital, ophthalmology, and dental industries. During this time, Alan held positions such as Process Engineer, Operations Manager, Director of Operations, Vice President, and Chief Strategy and Innovation Officer, with P&L responsibility over $100 million.

In his spare time, Alan enjoys spending time with his partner and son, swing dancing (Lindy Hop and Balboa), cycling, and cooking.
